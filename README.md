# DayFlow (Personal Schedule) 

DayFlow is a comprehensive, beautifully designed personal scheduling and productivity application built with a modern glassmorphic aesthetic. It helps you manage your daily tasks, track your long-term aspirations, and visualize your progress with powerful analytics.

## Features 

### 1. Timeline View 
- Plan your day with a clear, hour-by-hour visual timeline.
- Easily add tasks with specific start and end times.
- Supports untimed/all-day tasks.

### 2. List View 
- View all your scheduled tasks for the day in a clean list format.
- Sort tasks by Chronological Order, Priority, Category, or Status.
- Quick checkboxes to mark tasks as completed.

### 3. Analytics Dashboard 
- **Day Analysis:** Review your daily performance, focus categories, and time spent on tasks.
- **Month Analysis:** View your productivity over the month with a visual completion calendar.
- **Year Analysis:** See your long-term activity with a GitHub-style yearly contribution heatmap and track your longest streaks.

### 4. Goals & Dares 
Set and track your aspirations beyond just daily tasks:
- **Weekly Goals:** Focus on short-term milestones.
- **Monthly Goals:** Keep track of larger objectives.
- **Long Term Goals:** Your overarching life and career aspirations.
- **Dare of the Day :** Push yourself out of your comfort zone with daily challenges.

### 5. Goals Analytics 
A dedicated dashboard just for your goals:
- View your overall goal completion percentage.
- Track total goals completed vs. total dares conquered.
- Sleek, animated progress bars for Weekly, Monthly, Long Term goals, and Dares.

## Tech Stack 
- **Frontend:** HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), CSS3 (Modern Glassmorphism Design).
- **Storage:** LocalStorage for immediate data persistence (with SQLite hooks available).
- **No frameworks:** Pure, lightweight, and blazing fast.

## How to Run 
1. Clone this repository to your local machine.
2. Open `index.html` in your favorite modern web browser.
3. Start planning your day and conquering your goals!

## Theme Support 🌗
DayFlow includes a built-in toggle to seamlessly switch between a sleek **Light Mode** and a stunning **Dark Mode** to suit your preference and environment.
